# Recommended next experiments

1. Repeat each condition with at least three independent reaction replicates and report individual points plus mean and uncertainty.
2. Include reagent blanks, enzyme-free controls, substrate-free controls, and matched buffer/solvent controls for every condition.
3. Export raw spectrophotometer readings and record sample IDs, reagent lots, exact final reaction volumes, and final pH, CaCl₂, ethanol, and starch concentrations.
4. Rebuild the maltose standard curve with replicate standards, document blank correction, fit the calibration model, inspect residuals, and keep the raw data with the analysis.
5. Normalize enzyme input (protein concentration or validated activity units) before comparing fungal and salivary amylase.
6. Increase the starch range until an observed plateau is reached before estimating kinetic parameters; do not infer Km or Vmax from the current rising 0.1–0.5% range.
7. Verify pH with a calibrated meter at assay temperature and use buffer systems whose composition is controlled across the pH series.
8. Clarify whether CaCl₂ and ethanol labels are stock or final concentrations and report the final concentration in the reaction mixture.
